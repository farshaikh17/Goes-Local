site's GHL key has no opportunities permission. Eric (17 Nov): "We don't exactly use pipelines right now, so its fine."

  is left for a person ("both contacts hold a deal"). A 401/403 from the opportunities search no longer blocks the
  pair (counts as 0); any other failure still does. Appointments are still checked through the API.
