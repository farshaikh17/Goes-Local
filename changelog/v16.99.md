## Changes (worker)
- **Out-of-area listings are hidden from the site** (sync): an address ending in another US state's code or a Canadian
  province (`addrState`, compared with the city's CITY_STATE, nothing city-specific) is moved to the new table
  `area_hidden` and removed from `businesses`; GHL is not touched. Claimed, Plus and Pro listings always stay. A listing
  whose address is fixed comes back on the next sync. Admin → "Out-of-area listings (CSV)" (/admin/duplicates?area=1).
  Chains entered with a head-office address (Tire Kingdom NY, CVS RI, Firestone TN…) are hidden too.
- **Hidden duplicate copies are tagged `duplicate` in GHL** (cron, `dupTagBatch`, 100 per 5-minute run through GHL's
  bulk tag call, one at a time if that is refused). New column `dup_hidden.ghl_tagged` (0 to do, 1 tagged, 2 contact
  deleted in GHL). A CRM outage or bad token leaves rows to retry. When a hidden copy comes back (claimed), the tag is
  removed. Covers all 10,530 Miami / 863 Orlando hidden copies, so future double imports are flagged automatically.
- **Category:** when Lead Category isn't one of the site's categories and maps to Other, the sub-category decides
  (`mainOf(subField)`) if it maps to a real category.
