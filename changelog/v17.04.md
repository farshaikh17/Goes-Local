## How a pair is merged (`mergePair`)
GHL's API has no merge, so:
1. Master = most recent lastActivity (calls/texts/emails); tie → the one a rep worked (not tagged untouched); tie → the
   listing on the site.
2. If the contact to remove has opportunities or appointments, nothing happens: status "manual", listed on
   /admin/merge for a person to merge in GHL (keeps everything).
3. Backup in D1 `merge_log` (full contact JSON, notes, transcript) before any change.
4. Onto the master: empty native fields and assigned rep (native call), empty custom fields — note-like fields joined
   with " ||| " — (custom-field call), the other contact's tags (minus duplicate/untouched), every note
   ("[Merged from duplicate contact, note of <date>]"), and its calls/texts/emails as transcript notes (60 lines per
   note: date · type · direction · rep · call status/duration: text).
5. Delete the other contact; then set the email/phone it held on the master (GHL allows one contact per email/phone);
   remove "duplicate" (and "untouched" unless both had it); a note on the master explains the merge.
6. Site: the listing keeps its address — when the master is the hidden copy, the business row's ghl_id moves to it and
   it is refreshed; every D1 table with a ghl_id column (claims, owner_pending, slug_redirects, …) is re-pointed;
   dup_hidden rows are cleared.

## Running it
- /admin/merge — status, counts, pairs left for a person or failed.
- /admin/merge?dry=1&n=20 — preview (no changes): master choice, fields/notes/transcript counts, sample lines.
- POST /admin/merge?n=20 — merge a batch. POST /admin/merge?auto=1 — the 5-minute cron merges MERGE_BATCH (8) pairs
  per run; ?auto=0 stops it.
