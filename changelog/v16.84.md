# v16.84-shared — Google reviews on a listing: highest-rated first, 1–2 star left out

Eric (29 Oct, Quick and Clean Car Wash, Miami): "The reviews displayed here has that 1-star at the top. Can we re-order
these reviews not to have that 1-star at the top … Or remove the one shown."

## Change
- The up-to-5 Google reviews on a claimed/paid listing (GOOGLEREVIEWS, from gp_reviews) are shown highest rating first
  (Google's order kept within a rating), and reviews under 3 stars are left out while there is at least one review of
  3+ stars to show.
- Google's Places API policies (developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies) ask for "a clear
  notice that describes how reviews are being ordered and filtered" and recommend telling users reviews aren't
  verified by Google: the section now says "Highest-rated reviews shown first · read all N reviews on Google. Reviews
  aren't verified by Google; Google removes fake content when it finds it." The overall rating and review count are
  Google's, unchanged.

## Tests
- Local preview with five reviews (1, 5, 5, 4, 5 stars): shows 5, 5, 5, 4; the 1-star is gone; notice shown.
