# v17.02-shared — Contact us page (/contact) and "Report a problem with this listing"

Eric (13 Nov): "Build it" (mockup approved). A visitor (Winvest Management) wrote to Mianro because the site had no
contact page or address anywhere.

## Changes (worker)
- New page /contact (`CONTACTPAGE`): topic chips (a listing is wrong or closed / I own a business / Advertising —
  hidden when the city is free-only — / Press & partnerships / Something else), name, email, optional phone, optional
  business or page, message. Side box: add a business, claim, featured plans (not on free-only cities), report a
  listing. Thank-you message after sending. ContactPage + BreadcrumbList structured data. Not edge-cached.
- On send: the person's contact in GHL is found or created (never attached to a business contact: noBiz), tagged
  `contact-form` and `contact: <topic>`, the message saved as a note; new contacts get source "Goes Local contact
  form" (ghlFindOrCreateContact now takes `tags`/`source`). Email template `admin_contact_message` to the new
  "Messages from the Contact us page" list (Admin → Email templates; falls back like other lists); "a listing is wrong
  or closed" goes to the Edge cases list instead. Work runs after the reply (ctx.waitUntil), so the visitor never waits.
- Spam: hidden "website" field (filled → thank-you shown, nothing sent), Tor POSTs already refused, 3 messages per
  visitor per hour (CONTACT_PER_IP), 40 per hour for the whole site (CONTACT_PER_HOUR).
- Links: "Contact us" in every footer (Company); "🚩 Report a problem with this listing →" under the contact box on
  every listing page, opening /contact with the topic and the listing address filled in; /contact in the sitemap.
