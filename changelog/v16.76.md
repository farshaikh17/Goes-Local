# v16.76-shared — invites to an Owner Email keep the sales rep as sender

Found in the v16.75 live test on North Carolina (23 Oct). On a cold-call verification the Owner Email is usually different from the business contact's email. GHL refused the send through the business contact (`CONVERSATIONS_MSG_INVALID_EMAILTO`). `sendEmail` treated that as a rep-sender failure: it dropped the rep, then retried through the owner's own contact as "No Reply"/site sender. Now that error, like "Contact has no email", goes straight to the retry through the recipient's own contact, still as the assigned rep.

Also on the state sites: v16.75 (sales Verified held until the owner's first sign-in). Live test on NC: Listing Verified → claimed=0, tag awaiting-owner-login, owner_pending row, invite sent. The owner's first sign-in → claimed=1, tags claimed + recently claimed. The test business was deleted.
