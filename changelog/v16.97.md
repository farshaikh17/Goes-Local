# v16.97-shared — new listings verified by someone who doesn't look like the business are flagged to dev

Eric (3 Nov), on the remaining risk for brand-new listings (someone lists a business that isn't on the site yet and
verifies it with their own email and mobile): option 2 — flag it — "Not to sales but the default edge account of Dev1".

## Change
- When a held new listing goes live (activateOwner → publishListing), it is flagged if the owner's email isn't on the
  business's website domain (or there's no website) AND the owner's verified mobile isn't the business phone:
  GHL tag `check-owner`, a note saying why, and an "Edge cases" email (devAlert → admin_needs_dev, default the dev
  inbox) with a link to the GHL contact. The listing still goes live (owners aren't slowed down).

## Tests
- 94/94 local (the flag runs only after a real publish; live test after deploy).
