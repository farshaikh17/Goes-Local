# v16.78-shared — resend rules for the "Listing Verified" invite

Eric (27 Oct): sales fixed Muse Health & Beauty's broken Owner Email and re-triggered "Listing Verified" several
times, but nothing went out — the invite was sent once per business. "Yes, build the resend rules."

## Change (applyListingVerification, invite step)
- New column `businesses.claim_invited_to` (migrate): the address the last invite went to.
- The invite goes again when Listing Verification is set again and either
  - the Owner Email (or business email) is different from the last invite's address, or
  - more than 24 hours have passed since the last invite;
  but never once the owner has signed in from it (owner_pending.activated_at), and never more than once a day
  (re-triggers within 24 h to the same address do nothing, as before).
- An address that can't be real (`BADMAIL`: spaces, no domain, characters like "&" or "#") is not emailed: tag
  `owner-email-invalid` and a GHL note "The Owner Email … isn't a valid email address … Correct it and set Listing
  Verification again." The tag is removed when an invite is sent.
- Older listings (no `claim_invited_to` yet) use the 24-hour rule.
