## Changes
- **Owner details required (applyListingVerification).** A cold-call Verified (the held case from v16.75) checks
  Owner Name, Owner Phone and Owner Email on the business contact. If any is empty: no invite, no hold, tag
  `owner-details-missing` and a GHL note naming what's missing; sales fill them and set Listing Verification again.
  The tag comes off once they're complete. A field the account doesn't have isn't asked for.
- **First sign-in verifies the Owner Email (activateOwner).** Tag `owner-email-verified`, and the note says the address
  is verified by the emailed code.
- **No new contacts for owners.**
  - `bizContactFor`: an email that is a business's Owner Email, a held sales invite's address, or a business email maps
    to that business's contact. `ghlFindContactByEmail` falls back to it, so password sign-in, code sign-in, sign-up,
    password reset, profile and password pages all use the business contact.
  - `ghlFindOrCreateContact` uses that business contact before creating one; it keeps the business's own name/company
    and only fills an empty Owner Name.
  - Sign-in codes go to the address typed (not the business's main email), and the "add this address to the contact"
    step in sendEmail now works for any contact, so codes show on the business's Conversations tab.
  - Profile page on a business contact reads/writes Owner Name and Owner Phone, never the business's own name, phone or
    address.
  - Site claim form: the claim's role, notes and (when empty) Owner Name/Phone go on the business contact. Only a dispute
    of a listing someone already owns keeps the claimant on a contact of their own.
- **Remaining edge cases that still create a contact:** a brand-new business added through the site, a shopper or
  reviewer with no business, an advertise request from an unknown email, a claim dispute, and the last-resort email
  fallback when GHL refuses to add the address to the business contact.
