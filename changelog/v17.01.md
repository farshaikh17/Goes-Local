# v17.01-shared — "Related guides" picked by topic (SEO safeguard 3)

Eric (12 Nov), after the SEO audit and the safeguards mockup: "3" — automatic Related guides at the end of every post.
Includes everything in v17.00 (not deployed separately).

## Why
The audit found 16 posts linking to no other post and 6 with no links in. Posts already had a 3-card "Keep reading"
box, but it showed the 3 newest posts, so it gave Google no topical signal.

## Change (worker)
- The box is now "Related guides" and is chosen by `RELATEDPOSTS`: same neighbourhood (named in the title/address or
  linked in the post, `RELHOODS`, the city's own name doesn't count) 4 points, same blog sub-category 3, else same blog
  category 2, each shared subject word in the address 1 (max 3; light plural/-ing stemming, common words ignored).
  Guides the post already links to are scored ×0.4 so the box adds new links. Empty places are filled with the newest
  guides, so the box is never empty and never links to the post itself.
- Each card shows a small label (the neighbourhood, or the blog category). When the post is about a neighbourhood
  (now also when it is only named in the title), the third card is "All businesses in <neighbourhood> →".
- Nothing to set up; works for every city from its own posts and neighbourhoods.

## Checked
- Locally with Miami's 36 real posts: Brickell → Brickell shopping / Brickell med spas / All businesses in Brickell;
  general contractors → hire a contractor / roofing; auto repair → Aventura auto shop / repair costs / tire repair;
  Kendall HVAC → Kendall dining / hire a contractor / All businesses in Kendall; facials → beauty guides. Posts with no
  close match (free business listing) get the newest guides. Phone width: no overflow.
- 98/98 local tests (new T55).
