## Changes
- **List your business form (/add and /join, same ADDCARD):** "What do you do?" removed. Step 2 asks "Are you the
  owner of this business?" — Yes: their name, email, mobile; No, I'm adding it for the owner: their name, role and
  email plus the owner's name, email and mobile. Button: "Verify and go live" / "Send to the owner to verify".
- **New listings go live only after the owner's email + mobile check — the claim route, no new pages.** On submit the
  listing is held (owner_pending with the new claim_id column → the claims row), the GHL contact gets
  awaiting-owner-login and a note saying who added it, and the owner gets the new "listing_owner_verify" email. The
  owner signs up / signs in with the email code, then confirms the code texted to their mobile on "Confirm you're the
  owner"; only then activateOwner publishes it (publishListing) and the usual claimed tags / Owner Name / Owner Phone
  are set. If the owner filled the form, they go straight to sign-up ("Your listing is saved…"); otherwise the
  submitter sees "Almost there — the owner just needs to verify". 3-day reminder as for sales invites. The old
  instant-publish and business-phone code routes for new listings are gone (claims keep theirs). Owner invites from
  the form: max 4 per visitor per hour, then the team route as before.
- **Category at first sign-in:** the account page shows "What does <business> do?" (next to "Set a password") for an
  owner whose listing is in Other; saving sets Lead Category in GHL (custom-field-only call) and refreshes the listing.
- "takes about 3 minutes" / "Takes 3 minutes" / "3 min" → 1 minute everywhere; steps say the owner verifies.
- /join headline bigger (up to 92px).
- bizContactFor never maps an email to a held listing's placeholder id.
