## Changes
- **"Private — never shown on the directory" box** (`PRIVBOX`) at the top of: the owner's confirm page
  (/manage/confirm, after a GHL Listing Verified), the claim form, and the add-a-business form's owner section.
- **Team logins (Team tab in Manage listing, POST /manage/team).**
  - New table `business_team(ghl_id, email, added_by, added_at)`. The owner (Owner Email) adds or removes up to 10
    people; they get the `team_invite` email (on the business contact) with a sign-up/sign-in link for their own email.
  - `ownedIds` includes team rows, so they see and manage the listing. Only the owner can change the team.
  - No new GHL contact: `bizContactFor` maps a team email to the business contact. A team member's password is kept in
    KV (`tpw:<email>`, `teamOnly`/`setPasswordFor`) so it never overwrites the owner's Owner Credentials.
  - GHL copy: new contact field **Team Members** (Miami `5CBasvWQXtYo1AOpVeGb`, Orlando `G4kts2ND3XJtA7mwjGeK`) holds
    the list; a note on each add/remove. Tampa and the states need the field added by hand; until then only the note.
- **Year established required** on the add-a-business form, the claim form and the owner's confirm page (1800–this
  year, `YEAR`). Written to GHL "Year established" (claim/confirm: only when empty / on confirm).
- **Lead Source** (`setLeadSource`): "Website – <form> (<channel>)", e.g. "Website – Claim form (Google search)",
  from the visitor's first-touch cookie (gl_attr). Only fills an empty Lead Source. On: claim, add a business, sign up,
  advertise, quote request.
- **Competitors hidden** (`COMPETITOR`): sub-category or category says marketing / advertising agency / website or web
  designer / web development / SEO / search engine / social media / digital agency / public relations / software company
  / branding agency, or the name says marketing / SEO / web design / websites / digital agency / media agency. Kept off
  the site by sync and insertOne (table `competitor_hidden`), never paid listings, never a contact tagged
  `not-competitor`. Report: /admin/competitors (`?scan=1` re-checks now).
  - Tagged `not-competitor` by hand: Turno, Ranyan Janitorial Management Software (Miami); 407 Injury Helpline, Injury
    Assistance Network (Orlando).
