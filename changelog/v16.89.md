## Changes
- **/owners** — owner landing page for ads (working version; the designer's design replaces it later). Hero with the
  city's real counts (listings, owners already claimed), "Find your business to claim it" search (unclaimed first;
  claimed ones show "Already claimed · Log in"), "Not listed? Add your business free", 3 steps (find, email + text
  code, make it yours), free benefits, private-details box, FAQ, closing call to action. Not indexed by Google
  (X-Robots-Tag noindex), so the ad page doesn't compete with the site in search.
  - Ad link: `/owners?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=<name>`.
  - Same flow as every claim (Claim → /claim/start → codes → /manage/confirm phone check), so the same tracking:
    Pixel ViewContent on the landing page, Lead on the claim / add / sign-up form, CompleteRegistration when the
    owner finishes the email + phone check; GHL tags facebook-ad + fb-<step> from the first-touch cookie.
- **CITY_META_DOMAIN_VERIFY** (optional Variable): Meta's domain-verification code, printed as
  `<meta name="facebook-domain-verification">` on every page.
