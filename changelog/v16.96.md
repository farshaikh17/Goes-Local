# v16.96-shared — security fix: "List your business" can't take over an existing listing

Found while answering Eric (3 Nov): "what if someone pretends to be the owner?"

## Problem (introduced in v16.95)
When the phone typed into "List your business" matched a listing already on the site (unclaimed), v16.95 held that
existing listing for the "owner" typed into the form. Whoever then passed the owner check with their own email and
mobile became its owner — and business phone numbers are public. Before v16.95 this case needed a code sent to the
listing's own phone.

## Change
- An add whose phone matches a listing on the site now stops with "This business is already listed — Claim this
  listing" (→ /claim/start for that listing), where the code goes to the listing's own phone or email, then the owner
  check. Nothing is held for the form-filler.
- The phone match also checks our own listings in D1 (GHL's lookup can miss or return a person's contact first).
- Held listings from the form are always the new submission (claim_id set), never an existing listing.
