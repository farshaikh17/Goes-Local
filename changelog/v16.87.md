# v16.87-shared — owner phone check: text reaches any mobile (fix for v16.86)

Live test on Orlando (2 Nov, Eric's own mobile) with v16.86: "We couldn't text that number". GHL logs:
- `CONVERSATIONS_MSG_PHONE_MISMATCH` "Contact phone number does not match with the toNumber" — GHL only texts a
  contact's own main phone.
- Adding the number to the business contact failed: "This location does not allow duplicated contacts" (the number
  already belonged to a contact GHL made when Eric phoned the Orlando line).
- Also found: a PUT with `additionalPhones` **replaces the contact's main phone** (tried on the QA contact only, put
  back). v16.86 would have done that to a business whose number wasn't already taken. Removed.

## Change
- The code goes to: the business contact when the owner's mobile is its phone; else the contact that already has the
  number; else a small new contact for the owner's phone (owner's name, the business name as company, tag
  `owner-phone`, source "Goes Local owner phone check"). That last case is the only new contact this makes — GHL can't
  text a number without a contact that owns it. On the first text, notes on the business and on that contact link the
  two, so sales can see where the text is.
- The business contact's own phone is never changed.
