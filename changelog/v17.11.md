## Change (worker, /api/lead enquiry path; checks run before anything reaches GHL, the owner's inbox or phone)
1. Hidden bot-trap field `website`: filled → the usual "Enquiry sent" page, nothing sent.
2. The listing must be a claimed listing on this site (owner email set); its name comes from our records, not the form.
3. Real details: a name with at least two letters, a valid email (BADMAIL), and a message of 10+ letters/digits or a
   10-digit phone; a phone that isn't one is refused.
4. Cloudflare Turnstile ("Verify you are human", Managed mode), only when the site has BOTH the Variable
   `TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY` and the Secret `TURNSTILE_SECRET`. If Cloudflare's check can't be reached the enquiry goes
   through (the limits still apply); a failed or missing check is refused. The CSP allows challenges.cloudflare.com
   only on those sites; the page cache key includes the site key.
5. Limits: 3 enquiries an hour per visitor address, 10 an hour per listing, 60 an hour per site ("Please slow down").
